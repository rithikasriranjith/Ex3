# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of this project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, education, experience level, or any other personal characteristic.

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment where everyone can contribute and communicate freely.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
* Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
* Showing empathy and consideration toward other community members.
* Giving credit to others for their contributions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or deliberately offensive comments.
* Publishing private information about others without permission.
* Unwelcome sexual or personal attention.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the project's GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other official communication channels.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers will take reasonable steps to protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

Anyone who does not follow this Code of Conduct may be subject to appropriate action, including warnings, removal of contributions, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and impact of each violation when deciding how to respond.

Possible responses include:

1. **Correction** – A private or public reminder of the Code of Conduct.
2. **Warning** – A formal warning regarding unacceptable behavior.
3. **Temporary Restriction** – Temporary restriction from participating in project activities.
4. **Permanent Removal** – Permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers will apply these guidelines fairly and consistently while considering the nature and severity of the incident.
